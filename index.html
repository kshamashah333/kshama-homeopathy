    What happens at a first homeopathic consultation

    A first consultation is mostly a conversation. Expect questions about your main concern, but also about your sleep, appetite, energy, moods and daily routine, and about your health history and your family's. These details help your homeopath choose a remedy that suits you as an individual, not just your diagnosis.

    You do not need to prepare much. It helps to bring a list of your current medicines and any recent test results. At the end you will usually leave with a remedy and a plan for when to review progress. Please keep seeing your GP for any medical concern, and never stop prescribed medicine without their advice.

    Homeopathy and your GP, side by side

    Homeopathy is a complementary approach. It is not a replacement for medical diagnosis or emergency care. If you are thinking of trying it, tell your homeopath about every medicine and supplement you take.

    A good practitioner will ask what your doctor has said and will tell you when something needs medical attention. If a symptom is new, severe or getting worse, see your GP first. In an emergency, call 999.

    Questions people ask before they book.

    If yours is not here, email us and we will answer within one working day.

    Do you see patients online or in clinic?

    Both. You can visit the clinic after booking an appointment or consult by video from anywhere. Remedies can be delivered if you are consulting online. Home visits can also be arranged for patients who are unable to travel.

    Which languages do you offer?

    Consultations are available in English, Gujarati, Hindi and Marathi.

    How long is a first appointment?

    It can be up to 90 minutes. It is longer than a standard consultation, because we take your full history.

    How many appointments will I need?

    This depends on you and your concern. We review progress together at each visit and agree the next step, so you are never committed to a long course up front.

    Is it safe alongside my current medicines?

    Homeopathic remedies are highly diluted. Even so, tell us about everything you take, and tell your GP that you are seeing us.

    Can I bring my child?

    Yes. Children under 16 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian who stays for the whole session, as set out in our terms.

    What if I need to cancel?

    Please give at least 48 hours’ notice so you can be rescheduled or refunded. Shorter notice and missed appointments may incur a fee. See our Terms and Conditions.

    How is my information protected?

    Your details are used only to arrange and deliver your care, and are never sold. Read our Privacy Policy for the full details.

    Clinic medical disclaimer

    1. Informational purposes only

    All information, articles, blogs, and content published on this website are provided for general educational and informational purposes only. The text is not intended to be, nor should it be construed as, a substitute for professional conventional medical advice, clinical diagnosis, or medical treatment.

    2. No doctor-patient or practitioner-patient relationship

    Accessing this website, submitting an inquiry via contact or booking forms, or reviewing materials does not create a formal practitioner-patient or clinical relationship between you and this clinic. A formal relationship is only established upon complete formal booking, screening intake, and signed medical consent forms.

    3. Alignment with Faculty of Homeopathy standards

    Our practicing homeopaths hold active registration with the Faculty of Homeopathy (UK) or equivalent statutory/professional regulatory registries. In strict compliance with professional guidelines, we practice within our clear boundaries of competence. Homeopathy operates as a holistic and integrative system of medicine. It is designed to complement, not replace, necessary conventional clinical care. Patients are strictly advised never to disregard, delay, or stop seeking professional medical advice from their General Practitioner (GP) or other NHS medical specialists because of information read on this website.

    4. Emergency situations

    This website and its associated communication channels are completely unmonitored for urgent healthcare issues or acute medical emergencies. If you are experiencing a life-threatening medical emergency or require immediate assistance, do not rely on this website. Contact the emergency services immediately (dial 999 or 111 within the United Kingdom) or visit your nearest NHS Accident and Emergency (A&E) department.

    Privacy policy and cookies declaration

    1. Personal and special category data we process

    We securely collect and process: Identity Data (name, DOB), Contact Data (email, phone, address), and Special Category Medical Data (clinical history, symptoms, prescription logs).

    2. Legal basis for processing health data

    Under UK GDPR, health data constitutes special category information. We process this under Article 9(2)(a) (Explicit Consent) collected during medical intake, and Article 9(2)(h) for the provisioning of healthcare or treatment.

    3. Data security and confidentiality protocols

    In alignment with Faculty of Homeopathy guidelines on confidentiality and secrecy, all digital records are securely encrypted behind GDPR-compliant Electronic Health Record (EHR) databases with multi-factor authentication (MFA). Records are retained for the statutory UK medical periods (minimum 8 years for adults, or up to age 26 for minors) and are never sold or traded.

    4. Cookies and web analytics

    Essential functional cookies are deployed for scheduling calendars and secure payments. Optional marketing/tracking cookies are entirely blocked unless explicitly accepted via our cookie choice banner.

    Clinic terms and conditions

    1. Booking policy and financial transparency

    Consultations are booked online or via phone. Rates for intakes, follow-ups, and homeopathic remedies are fully transparent on our pricing portal. Payment is settled securely at the time of reservation.

    2. Notice of cancellation and no-show penalties

    We enforce a strict 48-hour cancellation policy. Cancellations with over 48 hours' notice are eligible for full refund or rescheduling. Cancellations under 48 hours incur a 50% session fee penalty. Direct no-shows forfeit 100% of the session fee.

    3. Remote and virtual video consultations

    When utilizing telehealth video consultation software, patients must occupy a secure, private room to safeguard privacy. The clinic is not liable for technical drops caused by local client network failures.

    4. Treatment of minors

    Minors under the age of 16 are legally restricted from booking sessions independently. A parent or legal guardian must provide documented consent and remain present throughout the entire session.

    5. Intellectual property

    All custom text, medical charts, research briefings, and content are the exclusive intellectual property of this clinic and cannot be reproduced without explicit written consent.

    Professional compliance and Faculty regulation

    1. Code of practice

    Our homeopaths are registered with the Faculty of Homeopathy (founded 1844, incorporated by Parliament 1950) or sister professional registries. They strictly abide by a rigorous Code of Professional Conduct.

    2. Continuing development

    Practitioners satisfy yearly Continuing Professional Development (CPD) metrics (ranging from 10 to 50 hours) audited under Faculty quality assurance schemes.

    3. Indemnity insurance

    Dr. Kshama holds professional, public and products liability insurance through the Faculty of Homeopathy scheme, underwritten by Zurich Insurance.

    4. Complaints strategy

    If any service drops below expectations, please notify us at our support desk. Unresolved matters may be formally escalated to the Faculty of Homeopathy council or relevant statutory registration bodies.
